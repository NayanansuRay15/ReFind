# Campus Lost & Found 2.0 🔍🎒

**Campus Lost & Found 2.0** is an automated web/mobile application designed to replace chaotic WhatsApp groups with an intelligent, centralized system that reconnects students with their missing belongings. 

By leveraging automated criteria-matching, the platform pairs lost item reports with found item reports, calculating a percentage-based match probability to help students retrieve their items quickly and securely.

---

## 🚀 The Problem & The Solution

### The Problem
Students constantly lose everyday essentials—ID cards, earphones, wallets, textbooks, water bottles, chargers, and keys. Currently, recovery relies on fragmented WhatsApp groups or Discord channels where messages quickly get buried and items are lost forever.

### The Solution
A dedicated digital hub where students can instantly report lost or found items. The system acts as an automated matchmaker:
* 🔴 **Lost something?** Upload a photo, specify the time/location, add a description, and submit.
* 🟢 **Found something?** Upload a photo, tag the location, and submit.
* 🔗 **The Magic:** The platform automatically compares the entries (e.g., *Lost: Black Boat earphones at the Library at 2:30 PM* vs. *Found: Black wireless earphones at the Library at 3:00 PM*) and alerts users with a **"Possible Match — 87%"** notification.

---

## ✨ Key Features

* **Instant Reporting:** Simple, user-friendly forms for both losing and finding items.
* **Smart Matching Engine:** Algorithmic matching based on item category, location tags, timestamps, and description keywords.
* **Image Uploads:** Visual verification to help users identify their exact property.
* **Secure Claim System:** Simple verification steps to ensure items return to their rightful owners.
* **Live Dashboard:** A clean, filtered feed of recently lost and found items across the campus.
