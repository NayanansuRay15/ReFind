How matching works

Every time you pin an item, we compare it against everything on the opposite list — lost against found, found against lost — and score the pair on four things:

  • Category — same kind of object
  • Location — same or nearby spot
  • Time — how close together they were reported
  • Description — shared words like color or brand

Pairs scoring 35% or higher show up under the Matches tab, strongest first. This board is shared — anything you pin is visible to everyone using it.
